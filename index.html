Hill Springs Academy

Hill Springs Academy is a private CBE school in Maua, Igembe South, Meru County, Kenya. We offer Kindergarten, Pre-Primary and Junior School under Kenya’s Competency-Based Education.

Building an excellent foundation for a brighter future. Schools in Maua, private schools in Maua, and families across Meru County are welcome to visit and enquire about admission.

Admissions: admissions@hillspringacademy.sc.ke. General: info@hillspringacademy.sc.ke.
